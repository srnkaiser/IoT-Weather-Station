## Table 3 - Statistical comparison

All models scored on the identical 83,959 forecasts, which is fewer than the test samples in Table 1: each sequence model additionally drops the first `seq_len` rows of the test portion, and the comparison is restricted to the timestamps every model can forecast. aligned on target timestamp. Diebold-Mariano on absolute-error differentials with a Newey-West (Bartlett) long-run variance over 144 lags (24 h), because forecast errors on a 10-minute series stay autocorrelated for hours; a test assuming independence would overstate every result. Confidence intervals are from a moving-block bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, block length 24 h. The reference model is the **median** of its three seeds, not the best.
